# Meeting artifact contract

The finished `MEETING.md` must contain:

1. Meeting metadata: title, date/time, source meeting id and participants when known.
2. Executive summary in five bullets or fewer.
3. Decisions, each with supporting transcript timestamps.
4. Action items in a table with owner, action, due date and evidence timestamp.
5. Risks, open questions and follow-ups.
6. A link/reference back to `TRANSCRIPT.md` and `ANALYSIS.md`.

Never invent a participant, decision, owner or due date. Use `Unassigned`, `Not stated`,
or `Unclear` when the transcript does not support a stronger claim.
